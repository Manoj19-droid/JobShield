🛡️ Job Safety Tips

01

Never Pay to Get Hired

Be cautious if someone demands registration fees or payment to guarantee a job.

02

Verify the Recruiter

Check the company's official website and careers page independently.

03

Question Unrealistic Offers

Unusually high salaries with no interview or experience requirements deserve scrutiny.

About JobShield

JobShield is a student mini-project designed to help users identify potential warning signs in job postings and application links. It combines job-description analysis with URL safety checks to help users make more informed decisions. No automated check can guarantee that a job or website is safe.
